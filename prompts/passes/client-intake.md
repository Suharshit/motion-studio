# Client intake — the questions that save a re-render

Ask only what the brief doesn't already answer. Put answers in `projects/<slug>/brief.md`. If the client is away, take the most reasonable reading, write it at the top of `brief.md` as **Assumptions**, and continue.

1. **What is it for?** Where does it play (Reels, YouTube pre-roll, landing page hero, keynote, paid ad)? → formats, length, safe areas.
2. **The one thing** the viewer must remember. One sentence.
3. **Subject material:** product URL, assets, data, script, track, references. What are we allowed to use? (Client confirms rights to anything they supply.)
4. **Brand:** colors (hex), fonts, logo (SVG), banned looks, a vault style they liked.
5. **Sound:** supplied track / synthesized score / none · voiceover (Fish Audio voice tone, language) · captions on/off.
6. **Deliverables + deadline:** formats, poster frame, captions .srt, source handoff, number of revision rounds (default: 2).

Default when unanswered: 20s · 9:16 + 1:1 + 16:9 · synthesized music · captions on if voiced · 2 revision rounds.
